## The new rules

| Old | New |
|---|---|
| 50 min dense teaching → 90 min lab → 25 min scored quiz | **40 min teaching ONE concept → 100 min practice with me watching → 25 min recap → 15 min 5-question check** |
| Several ideas per day | **One idea per day. Never two.** |
| Quiz scored /100 every day, strict | 5 low-stakes questions. The score measures the teaching, not you. |
| 10-question exam every Saturday, pass 70 | 30 min, 10 questions, **open-notes, pass 60** |
| Nothing repeated | **Every 5th day is a practice day** — no new material at all |

**The most important new rule:** if you say "too fast", the session stops and backs up. It does not push on. Say it early and say it often — that sentence is the single most useful thing you can contribute to this process.

**Second most important:** a wrong answer beats a blank one, always. On Day 1 you lost 32 marks to blank answers. Under the new shape you'll be writing code with me watching, so there's nowhere for a blank to hide — but the principle still holds. Guess out loud.
