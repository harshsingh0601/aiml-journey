**Answered:** `12`, `5`. Reason: *"because b = a, happened when the value of a was 5 and the

**Marking:** Full marks — output correct and the mechanism stated exactly. Assignment hands
over a **value**, it does not link two names. Output alone was not enough for the marks here;
